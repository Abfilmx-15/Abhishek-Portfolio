HOW TO PUT THIS WEBSITE ONLINE
==============================
Your hosting must support PHP (almost all shared hosting does, e.g. Hostinger, GoDaddy, Bluehost, Namecheap).

1. Log in to your hosting control panel and open "File Manager".
2. Open the folder called public_html (sometimes "www" or "htdocs").
3. Upload abhishek-portfolio.zip there and choose "Extract".
4. Open the extracted folder and move EVERYTHING inside it (index.html, the admin, api, css, js, data, uploads, assets
   and google-sheets folders) directly into public_html. Make sure index.html sits directly in public_html.
5. Right-click the folders "data" and "uploads", choose Permissions, and set 755 (or 775 if saving fails).
6. Visit yourdomain.com for the site and yourdomain.com/admin/ for the Admin Panel.

FIRST LOGIN (do this immediately)
Username: admin     Password: Admin@123
The panel opens on "Admin password". Enter Admin@123 as the current password, optionally choose a new username, then a new
password of at least 8 characters, and click "Update login details".

SAVE CONTACT FORM MESSAGES IN GOOGLE SHEETS (one-time setup, ~3 minutes)
1. Create a new Google Sheet at sheets.google.com.
2. In the sheet click Extensions > Apps Script. Delete the sample code, paste everything from the file
   google-sheets/google-apps-script.gs, and click Save.
3. Click Deploy > New deployment > type "Web app". Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy and approve the permissions.
4. Copy the Web app URL (ends in /exec).
5. In the Admin Panel open "Contact form -> Google Sheets", paste the URL, click "Save address", then "Send test row".
   A test row should appear in your sheet. From then on every contact form message becomes a new row.
(Until this is set up, the form falls back to opening the visitor's email app.)

Tip: don't link to the admin page anywhere public, and never share your password.

THE ADMIN PANEL WON'T OPEN OR LOG IN?
- Do NOT double-click admin/index.html on your computer. The panel needs a web server with PHP, so it only works online
  on your hosting, or locally through XAMPP (put the folder in xampp/htdocs, start Apache, then open
  http://localhost/abhishek-portfolio/admin/ in your browser).
- Online, the address is yourdomain.com/admin/ (with the trailing slash). If you see "404 Not Found", the admin folder
  was not moved into public_html. If you see a page of code, your hosting does not have PHP switched on - enable PHP in the
  hosting control panel (or ask their support).
