The story behind the edits

I started editing two years ago as a hobby, while studying electronics engineering. It began small: cutting a college event video and putting together content for my own profile.

Electronics gave me an unexpected head start. I was used to thinking in signals, from sine waves on a bench to audio waveforms on a timeline, and I fell in love with how sound, colour and pacing shape the way people feel about a video.

That curiosity grew into editing, sound design and colour grading, and then into a full-time career. What makes me different is that I don’t just cut clips together. I build a narrative that keeps the viewer watching until the very end.

My mission is to help creators and brands turn raw, unorganized footage into polished stories that grow their audience.
